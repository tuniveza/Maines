Playing an alt? Let your friends know it's you.

Maines puts your main character's name in front of everything you say in chat.

Your guild sees who is really talking, whichever character you're on.

[Guild] [Linenseller]: [Tuniveza] anyone up for a key?

No more "who is this?" in guild chat.

Set it up in 10 seconds

  1. Type /mains YourMainName [
  2. Log in on an alt and chat as normal.

That's it. Your tag appears on every message.

On your main, Maines stays quiet, because everyone already knows it's you.

What you get

🏷️ Your name, your style Pick a bracket: [Tuniveza] (Tuniveza) <Tuniveza> {Tuniveza}, or any symbol you like.

✨ A tag that moves Your tag can change with each message. It can type itself out, roll a capital letter through your name, or twinkle.

💬 Saved phrases on one key Keep the things you say every night, like "ty for the group!". Press your key and the phrase appears in the chat box. You still press Enter to send.

🛡️ Textual Badges Send a whole picture made of text: a heart, a sword, a crown, a murloc and more. You can also draw your own. You can send one every 3 minutes.

📻 Choose your channels Tag guild, party, raid, whispers, communities and more. Turn off any you don't want.

🎨 Paint the window Repaint the settings window with colours from famous paintings, or from Gnomeregan and Undermine.

Prefer buttons to commands?

Type /maines, or click the brass M on your minimap.

Everything can be done from the settings window.

Handy commands

Type this To do this
/mains Name [ Set your main's name and bracket
/maines Open the settings window
/mainphrase add <text> Save a phrase
/mainanim Make your tag move
/mainbadge See and send badges
/mainhelp See every command, with examples

Questions

Does it work on my main? It stays quiet there by default. Type /mainshow if you want the tag on your main too.

My tag isn't showing. Type /main to check your name is set. Then type /mainchat to make sure every channel is tagged again.

Does it send messages for me? Never. Maines only adds your tag to messages you send yourself.
